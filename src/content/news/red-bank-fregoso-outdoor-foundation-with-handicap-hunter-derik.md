<p>Red Bank and the Fregoso Outdoor Foundation took Derek on his first hunt &mdash; a wild boar hunt. With the help of a specially designed gun, Derek set out to take his first hog, accompanied by his dad. An exciting day for the Jellison family.</p>

<p>It was an eighteen-month road to get here, from hunter&rsquo;s safety through testing the gun &mdash; but we are on our way.</p>
