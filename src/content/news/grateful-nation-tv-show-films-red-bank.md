<p>Tim Abell, host of the Outdoor Channel&rsquo;s <em>Grateful Nation</em>, filmed at Red Bank with guest shooter SFC Raymond Castillo, a soldier who suffered a double-amputation injury while serving in Iraq.</p>

<p>Raymond showed everyone how to overcome life-altering changes through determination and perseverance while hog hunting with the Red Bank staff. &ldquo;Truly amazing,&rdquo; said ranch manager Ric Gould, guiding him up a steep hill.</p>
