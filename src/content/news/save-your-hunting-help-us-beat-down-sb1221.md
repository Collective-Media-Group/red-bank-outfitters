<p>In 2012 Red Bank joined the coalition of sportsmen&rsquo;s groups opposing SB&nbsp;1221, the proposed California hunting ban, alongside the U.S. Sportsmen&rsquo;s Alliance.</p>

<p>The bill and the fight over it are part of the ranch&rsquo;s history of standing up for California hunters.</p>
